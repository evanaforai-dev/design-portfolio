internal tooling · airtribe

vision

moving payments out of a google sheet and into airtribe's internal sales and operations product, in three phases, without the sheet ever going dark.

role
product designer
team
four: two product designers, one backend, one frontend
product
vision, internal sales and operations
year
2026

the brief was to replace the sheet. what shipped keeps a row index into it. the payment record in vision still carries whether it is synced, which row it came from, and whether that row has since been deleted, which means the sheet was never killed, it was made a peer. most of the design work is in that gap: a product that has to be better than a spreadsheet while still agreeing with one.

context

vision is airtribe's internal operations dashboard. sales, post-sales, finance, learner delivery, support and people all run out of it, and none of it is learner-facing. payments sit at the seam: the sales team records what a learner paid and how, and everything downstream, enrolment, slack access, onboarding, invoicing, keys off that record.

before this work, that record was a google sheet. it was fast, everyone could see it, and it had no idea who was allowed to change what.

the question

what does a product have to do before someone gives up a spreadsheet they trust?

what one payment record has to hold

a payment is not an amount. it is an amount, the instrument it arrived by, where that instrument currently is, and what each downstream team is allowed to do next. six payment types and eleven statuses, and the statuses are not a pipeline, they are the states a real payment gets stuck in.

that is the density problem in one sentence. the screen has to show a row that spans sales, finance and delivery, to people who each only care about their own third of it.

paid in one shot
→ cleared, or still pending verification
part payment
→ part paid by airtribe, or awaiting the rest
credit card, no cost emi
→ will pay by credit card, then cleared
credit card, cost emi
→ same path, different maths
loan, no cost emi
→ pushed for loan, documents, approval, disbursement
loan, cost emi
→ or rejected, which is its own state and not a failure of the form

the record also carries slack access state, onboarding status, an lxd comment and dashboard access status. those are phase 2: other teams' columns, living inside a sales record, because that is where the sheet had put them.

the decisions the screen had to make

  1. 01

    sync is a state, not a success message

    reconciling with the sheet resolves six ways: a row was created, the sheet was pulled in, it was already in sync, it was not found or not permitted, it failed, or it was ambiguous. ambiguous is the one that matters. a person at 6pm has to be told which record they are looking at and which one they are not.

  2. 02

    a rejected loan is a state, not an error

    loan rejected, access removed, awaiting documents and waiting for disbursement are all ordinary places a payment sits. designing them as error states would have told the agent something had gone wrong with their work rather than with the payment.

  3. 03

    the row belongs to three teams

    sales writes it, finance reconciles it, delivery acts on it. the hierarchy had to let each of them find their own third without hiding the other two, because the sheet never hid anything and that was half of why people trusted it.

email templates, for managers and agents

the same product, a different muscle. templates are what the sales team sends from, and the design question is not the editor, it is the line between the two roles: what a manager can author and what an agent can only send. it shipped alongside a separate piece of work bucketing permissions by role, which is the same question asked at the level of the whole product.

filters and search, include and exclude

an opportunity in vision carries about twenty five fields across three lifecycle axes that people constantly mistake for one: a status, a stage, and a separate lead status. filtering is where that confusion becomes expensive, because a query that mixes them silently returns the wrong queue. the redesign added include and exclude to search, and a course and cohort filter built for the same dataset.

a spreadsheet's real advantage is not that it is flexible. it is that nobody has ever had to ask it for permission.

outcome

all three phases shipped into vision - sales, alongside the email template flow, the course and cohort filter and the filters and search redesign.
